# Tax field reference (US · UK · Australia · Canada)

A quick reference for which tax label and ID to put on a business document in each country. This is general information to help you fill in the templates, **not** tax advice — check your own obligations or ask an accountant.

## United States 🇺🇸
- **Tax label:** Sales Tax (varies by state and sometimes city/county).
- **Rate:** set per the buyer's location; there is no single national rate.
- **Business ID:** EIN (Employer Identification Number) for most businesses.
- **Invoice note:** show the sales-tax rate and amount as a separate line when you charge it.

## United Kingdom 🇬🇧
- **Tax label:** VAT (Value Added Tax). Standard rate 20% (reduced and zero rates exist).
- **Business ID:** VAT registration number (only if VAT-registered).
- **Invoice note:** a VAT invoice must show your VAT number, the VAT rate, and the VAT amount per line or in total.

## Australia 🇦🇺
- **Tax label:** GST (Goods and Services Tax), 10%.
- **Business ID:** ABN (Australian Business Number).
- **Invoice note:** a "Tax Invoice" over the threshold must say *Tax Invoice*, show your ABN, and state the GST amount (or that the total includes GST).

## Canada 🇨🇦
- **Tax label:** GST/HST, plus PST or QST in some provinces.
  - GST 5% federally; HST (combined) in several provinces; PST/QST added separately in others.
- **Business ID:** GST/HST registration number (Business Number).
- **Invoice note:** show the tax type(s), rate(s), amount(s), and your registration number.
