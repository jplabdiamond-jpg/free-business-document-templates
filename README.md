# Free Business Document Templates

Free, ready-to-use templates for the paperwork small businesses and freelancers deal with every day — **invoices, quotes/estimates, receipts, purchase orders, delivery notes and credit notes**. Every template is plain text (Markdown, HTML and CSV), so you can open it anywhere, copy it, version it in Git, or paste it into your own tools. No sign-up, no account, no watermark.

These templates are maintained alongside **[Free BizDoc](https://free-bizdocs.com/en/)** — a free, no-sign-up web app that turns a few fields into a clean PDF, with sales tax / VAT / GST for the US, UK, Australia and Canada built in. If you'd rather fill a form and download a finished PDF than edit a template by hand, use the live generator at **<https://free-bizdocs.com/en/>**.

## How to use these templates

1. **Pick a template** from the table above and open the format you like (Markdown is the easiest to edit).
2. **Replace everything in `{{ curly braces }}`** with your own details — your business, your customer, the line items and the totals.
3. **Save and send.** For a PDF, either print the HTML version to PDF from your browser, or paste your details into the [live generator](https://free-bizdocs.com/en/invoice) and download a formatted PDF in seconds.

> Tip: keep a copy of your filled-in templates in a private repo or folder so each invoice number stays unique and your records are easy to search.

## Why plain-text templates?

- **Portable** — they open in any editor, on any OS, with no special software.
- **Versionable** — track changes to your standard wording in Git.
- **Automatable** — the CSV version drops straight into a spreadsheet or a script.
- **Private** — nothing leaves your machine until *you* send it.

If you need calculations done for you (line totals, tax, withholding, multi-currency), or a polished PDF without touching HTML, that's exactly what [Free BizDoc](https://free-bizdocs.com/en/) does for free in the browser — your data is generated client-side and isn't stored on a server unless you choose to save it.
